## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Global Ads Performance Dataset

This dataset contains advertising campaign performance across **Google Ads, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads**, covering multiple industries, campaign types, and countries.

### Dataset Overview

- **Platforms:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads
- **Industries:** Fintech, EdTech, Healthcare, E-commerce, SaaS
- **Campaign Types:** Search, Video, Shopping, Display
- **Countries:** USA, UK, Canada, Germany, UAE
- **Time Period:** 2024
- **Currency:** USD ($)

### Features

- Platform
- Campaign Type
- Industry
- Country
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Conversions
- Ad Spend
- Revenue
- CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- CPC (Cost Per Click)
- CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Goal |
|---------|---------|------|
| **CTR** | Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100 | Higher |
| **CPC** | Ad Spend ÷ Clicks | Lower |
| **CPA** | Ad Spend ÷ Conversions | Lower |
| **ROAS** | Revenue ÷ Ad Spend | Higher |

The dataset was used to compare platform performance, perform statistical hypothesis testing, identify selection bias, and generate business recommendations.
